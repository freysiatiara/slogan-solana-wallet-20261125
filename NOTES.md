## Gate limits

Program writes one slogan to `/output/entry.txt`: UTF-8 text, 1–140 characters, not only whitespace, single line, at most 20 words.

## Judge criteria (copy chief, head-to-head, creativity first, clarity second)

1. Clearly a slogan for a Solana wallet agent — names or evokes that wallet.
2. Fresh image, joke or turn of phrase — NOT a stock crypto line about keys, moons or bags.
3. Promise clear to a newcomer on one read, plain words, no jargon.
4. Rhythm/contrast — sticks in the ear, easy to repeat.
5. Honest — promises nothing it cannot deliver.

Tie-break: smaller text wins exact ties, then earlier receipt.

## Candidates (drafted and judged in-session)

1. "Solana in your pocket, a wallet that answers when you do." (60 chars) — flat; "in your pocket" is a stock wallet line, no fresh image.
2. "Your Solana wallet, awake before you are." (43 chars) — cute but vague; "awake" promises little a newcomer can grasp.
3. "Ask your wallet. It answers in Solana." (38 chars) — CHOSEN.
4. "The wallet that talks back in Solana." (40 chars) — close second; same image as 3 but passive, no inviting verb.
5. "Solana spending, sorted before you finish the sentence." (61 chars) — wordy, "spending, sorted" is clunky, jargon-adjacent.
6. "Your Solana wallet, with a mind for your money." (51 chars) — "mind for your money" is a cliché turn, not fresh.
7. "Talk to your Solana wallet. It handles the rest." (52 chars) — clear, but "handles the rest" is vague about what the agent does.
8. "A Solana wallet that listens, then does the doing." (53 chars) — "does the doing" is awkward phrasing.

## Head-to-head reasoning

- 3 vs 4: same talking-wallet image; 3 wins on criterion 4 — the ask/answer Q&A contrast is a two-beat rhythm that sticks in the ear, and the active verb "Ask" invites the newcomer in.
- 3 vs 7: both clear and honest; 3 wins on brevity (38 vs 52 chars — tie-break favours smaller text) and on criterion 2 (a wallet that answers is a fresher image than "handles the rest").
- 3 vs 2: 2 is shorter-sounding but fails criterion 3 — "awake before you are" says nothing concrete about the wallet's promise; 3 states it plainly.
- 3 vs 5/6/8: all lose on criterion 2 or 4 (stock phrasing, clunky rhythm).

## Chosen entry

> Ask your wallet. It answers in Solana.

- Characters: 38
- Bytes (UTF-8): 38
- Words: 7
- Single line: yes

Mechanism: two beats — a question ("Ask your wallet") answered in kind ("It answers in Solana"). The fresh image is a wallet you talk to, not a vault you guard; it names Solana directly, uses only plain words, and promises exactly what a wallet agent does: answer.
